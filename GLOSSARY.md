# The Lantern Below
A beacon keeper's expedition beneath a coastal refuge.

## Language
**Refuge**: The safe harbor where the keeper prepares and restores the beacon.
_Avoid_: town, base

**Seal**: One of three irreplaceable fragments needed to restore the beacon.
_Avoid_: key, crystal

**Beat**: One committed action followed by the inhabitants' responses.
_Avoid_: tick, round

**Bellkeeper**: The last guardian of the submerged beacon works.
_Avoid_: final monster

**Expedition**: The persistent journey through the three depths and back to refuge.
_Avoid_: run, match
